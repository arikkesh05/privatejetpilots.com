# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — Sovereign Flight Decks

## 1. Product Overview
Sovereign Flight Decks is an ultra-luxury, high-contrast, editorial private aviation charter and fleet intelligence web platform inspired directly by the Jesko Jets (jeskojets.com) aesthetic. It targets ultra-high-net-worth individuals (UHNWI), family offices, corporate flight departments, and executive principals who demand discretion, precision telemetry, and instant charter routing.

## 2. Problem Statement
Most aviation charter websites are either cluttered broker forms or tacky "cyberpunk video games" with scanlines and loud alarms. Sovereign delivers a razor-sharp, whisper-quiet, editorial digital atelier with real-time avionics telemetry, bespoke charter estimation, and sovereign ground concierge integration.

## 3. Scope & Target Audience
- **Target Audience**: Global business principals, flight coordinators, sovereign asset managers.
- **Tone & Mood**: Haute luxury, cinematic, editorial, quiet confidence, obsidian black contrast, champagne gold accents.
- **Supported Platforms**: Desktop (1440px+ optimized), Tablet, and Mobile (390px responsive).

## 4. Core User Flows & Features
1. **Floating Sovereign Dock**: Minimalist glassmorphic header dock with quick links (Fleet, Charter, Telemetry, Concierge), tactile click sound toggle, and high-priority "Request Flight" CTA.
2. **Cinematic Hero**: Dusk stratosphere Gulfstream imagery, editorial typography, zero scanlines, and an inline 5-column Mission Dispatch Quick-Quote Bar (Origin, Destination, Date, PAX, Jet Class).
3. **Sovereign Heavy Jet Bento**: Editorial three-aircraft showcase (Gulfstream G700, Bombardier Global 7500, Dassault Falcon 10X) featuring real cabin photography, technical flight envelopes, and charter deployment triggers.
4. **Real-Time Avionics Suite**: Clean 3D WebGL radar flight envelope viewer, live synthetic route arc, satellite broadband indicators, and cabin pressurization metrics.
5. **Executive Concierge Protocols**: Three editorial pillars: Zero-Knowledge Manifest Encryption, Direct Armored Tarmac Transfers, and Michelin In-Flight Atelier.
6. **Fullscreen Cockpit HUD Modal**: Activated via hotkey `[O]`, delivering clean tactical telemetry overlays with zo page scroll bleed.
7. **Whisper-Soft Web Audio Engine**: 100% silent background. Whisper-soft tactile click sounds trigger exclusively on explicit user interactions.
